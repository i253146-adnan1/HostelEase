## Architecture & Module Design
- `hostel.models`:
  - `Student`: `student_id` (unique UK), `full_name`, `contact_number`, `guardian_name`, `guardian_contact_number`, `email`, `address`, `status` (Active/Left), `user_id` (logical reference to User).
  - `Room`: `room_number` (unique UK), `capacity` (>= 1).
  - `RoomAssignment`: `room` (FK), `student_id` (logical student ref), `is_active`, `assigned_at`.
- `hostel.room_service`:
  - Encapsulates Room Catalogue & Capacity invariant (BR-06, VS-03 contract).
  - `assign_student`, `list_rooms`, `get_room`, `create_room`, `update_room`, `get_occupancy`, `get_student_room`, `clear_student_room`.
  - Transactional locking with `select_for_update()` to prevent race-condition overcapacity.
- `hostel.student_service`:
  - `register_student`: Atomic transaction (`@transaction.atomic`) coordinating:
    1. Mandatory field checks & contact number validation (10–15 digits).
    2. Student ID uniqueness check against Student and User.
    3. Money parsing & validation for `total_fee` and optional `opening_paid`.
    4. Room assignment & capacity check via `room_service.assign_student`.
    5. User account creation with `must_change_password=True` and one-time temporary password.
    6. Student record creation.
    7. Fee record & initial audit history creation via `finance.services.create_fee_record`.
    8. Complete transactional rollback on any failure (OQ-21, EC-30, EC-31).
  - `exists(student_id)`: Student identity verification for other modules (VS-03, Visitor Log EC-49).
- Views & Templates:
  - `warden_student_list`: Overview table with student info, assigned room, and fee ledger summary.
  - `warden_student_create`: Form with mandatory fields, room selector (with capacity/occupancy indicators), total fee, and opening balance.
  - `warden_student_success`: Credentials handover display with generated temporary password and security alert.
  - `warden_student_detail`: Full resident profile, room assignment, fee overview, and immutable audit history.
  - `warden_dashboard.html`: Updated Student Management link.
