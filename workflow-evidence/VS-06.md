## Architecture & Module Design
- `hostel.models`:
  - `Announcement`: `title`, `message`, `created_at`, `updated_at`; `clean()` rejects empty or whitespace-only title/message.
  - `MenuItem`: `day_of_week` (`Monday`–`Sunday`), `meal_type` (`Breakfast`, `Lunch`, `Snacks`, `Dinner`), `items`, `created_at`, `updated_at`; `clean()` rejects empty or whitespace-only items.
  - `Rule`: `title`, `description`, `created_at`, `updated_at`; `clean()` rejects empty or whitespace-only title/description.
  - Removal policy (`BR-05`): Announcements, menu entries, and rules are not protected by the never-delete rule and may be removed by the warden per PRD (`FR-12`).
- `hostel.views` & `hostel.urls` (included under `/hostel/` in `config/urls.py`):
  - Forms: `AnnouncementForm`, `MenuItemForm`, `RuleForm` using `_clean_non_empty` to strip and reject blank/whitespace-only inputs.
  - Shared CRUD helpers: `_handle_info_form` and `_handle_info_delete`.
  - Student read-only views: `student_announcements`, `student_menu`, `student_rules` rendering `templates/hostel/student_announcements.html`, `student_menu.html`, and `student_rules.html`.
  - Warden CRUD views: `warden_announcements`, `announcement_create`, `announcement_edit`, `announcement_delete`, `warden_menu`, `menu_create`, `menu_edit`, `menu_delete`, `warden_rules`, `rule_create`, `rule_edit`, `rule_delete` guarded by `_warden_required` (returns HTTP 403 Forbidden for non-warden/student users), rendering `templates/hostel/warden_*.html`, `entry_form.html`, and `confirm_delete.html`.
  - Public home page (`templates/home.html`): Does not display announcements, menu entries, or rules (`OQ-02` default).
