## Architecture & Module Design
- `operations.models.Feedback`:
  - Fields: `student_id` (logical reference string, no cross-module FK), `food_rating` (1–5), `room_rating` (1–5), `washroom_rating` (1–5), `environment_rating` (1–5), `created_at`.
  - Immutability constraint (BR-11): `save()` raises `PermissionError` on existing instances.
  - Retention constraint (BR-05): `delete()` raises `PermissionError` to prevent hard deletion.
- `operations.feedback_service`:
  - `submit_feedback`: Validates 1–5 star integer constraint for all 4 aspects; persists feedback record. Repeat submissions allowed (AC-15.3).
  - `get_feedback_summary`: Aggregates total submissions, counts per star level (1 to 5) for each aspect, percentages, and average ratings (AC-15.4). Handles empty state (0 submissions) without division-by-zero errors.
  - `list_student_feedback`: Retrieves read-only history of submissions for a given student.
- Views & Templates:
  - `student_feedback_view`: Student form with 1–5 star radio selectors and past submission history table.
  - `warden_feedback_summary_view`: Warden analytics overview displaying breakdown bars and average ratings for Food, Rooms, Washrooms, and Environment.
  - Dashboard integration: Updated `student_dashboard.html` and `warden_dashboard.html` navigation links.
