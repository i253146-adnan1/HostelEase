## Architecture & Decision Summary
- **Governance (`decision-log.md`, Log 26 — OQ-04, OQ-05 & partial OQ-24):**
  - Room swaps require explicit in-app consent from the target student (`PENDING_STUDENT_CONSENT`) before entering the warden's approval queue (`PENDING_WARDEN_APPROVAL`), or terminating as `REJECTED_BY_STUDENT` if declined.
  - Room-change requests use `PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `CANCELLED`.
  - Extends BR-04's listed statuses for the `SWAP` sub-type; flagged for client/TA confirmation.
  - Approved swaps take effect immediately and atomically inside `@transaction.atomic` with `select_for_update()`.
  - **OQ-24 / EC-42:** `EC-41` (room full) and `EC-43` (assignment/capacity conflict before swap) are re-validated atomically under `select_for_update()` at approval time. `Room` in VS-03 has no soft-delete/deactivation status field (`RoomRequest.preferred_room` uses `on_delete=models.PROTECT`, blocking DB deletion while referenced), so full soft-removal handling for `EC-42` remains **out of scope / still open** under OQ-24.
- **Models (`hostel/models.py`, migration `0003_roomrequest_roomswapnotification`):**
  - `RoomRequest`: `student_id` (logical `Student.student_id`, no cross-module FK), `request_type` (`CHANGE`, `SWAP`), `preferred_room` (`ForeignKey(Room, on_delete=PROTECT)`), `target_student_id`, `reason`, `status`, `warden_remark`, `created_at`, `updated_at`, `decided_by_student_at`, `decided_by_warden_at`.
  - `RoomSwapNotification`: `recipient_student_id`, `room_request`, `notification_type` (`SWAP_INCOMING`, `SWAP_ACCEPTED_BY_TARGET`, `SWAP_REJECTED_BY_TARGET`, `SWAP_APPROVED`, `SWAP_REJECTED_BY_WARDEN`), `message`, `is_read`, `created_at`.
  - `Room.get_pending_request_count()` updated per **ADR-005** to count `CHANGE (PENDING)` and `SWAP (PENDING_WARDEN_APPROVAL)` only (excluding `PENDING_STUDENT_CONSENT`).
- **Module Boundary (`hostel/student_service.py`):**
  - Added `validate_active_student(student_id: str) -> dict | None` and `StudentService.validate_active_student(...)` so `hostel/services.py` and `hostel/views.py` never query `Student` directly.
- **Business Logic (`hostel/services.py`):**
  - `get_available_rooms()`, `submit_change_request()`, `submit_swap_request()`, `respond_to_swap()`, `cancel_request()`, `decide_change_request()`, `decide_swap()`, and `get_pending_request_count()`.
- **Views, URLs & Templates (`hostel/views.py`, `hostel/urls.py`, `templates/hostel/`):**
  - Student views (`student_room_requests`, `student_room_request_create`, `student_room_request_cancel`, `student_swap_inbox`, `student_swap_respond`) guarded by `student_required` (`accounts.guards`) with strict ownership/target enforcement (`NFR-01` / `BR-08`).
  - Warden views (`warden_room_requests`, `warden_decide_room_request`) guarded by `warden_required` (`accounts.guards`).
