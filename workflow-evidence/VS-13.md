## 1. Scope

Warden-only complaint list (with status filters), complaint detail view (including attachment download via VS-08), forward-only status transitions (`Submitted → In Progress → Resolved`), mandatory remark when marking `Resolved` (OQ-12), no reopen action (OQ-03), and student read-only visibility of updated status and remark.
