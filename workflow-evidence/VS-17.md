## Architecture & Decision Summary
- **Governance (`decision-log.md`, Log 29 — OQ-32 & Existence Validation):**
  - **OQ-32 (Retention & No Delete):** Visitor-log entries are retained; no delete operation is offered in this slice. This is the conservative default under BR-05 pending a confirmed retention period — flagged for client/TA confirmation, not a final answer to how long entries are kept.
  - **Student Existence-Only Validation:** FR-18/AC-18.1–AC-18.4 require the referenced student to exist, but do not state the student must be `Active` (unlike VS-11's swap target or VS-15's leave requester). This slice validates student *existence* only, not `Active` status, since a visitor could plausibly be recorded against a student who has since been marked `Left`. Flagged for confirmation with the client/TA rather than silently assuming `Active`-only.
- **App Placement (`operations`):**
  - Placed in the `operations` app alongside `Feedback`, `Complaint`, `StoredFile`, and `LeaveRequest`, with all routes isolated strictly under `operations/warden/visitors/` and zero student-facing URLs, views, or navigation links (`BR-13`).
- **Model (`operations/models.py`, migration `0005_visitorlogentry`):**
  - `VisitorLogEntry`: `visitor_name` (`CharField(max_length=150)`), `student_id` (`CharField(max_length=50, db_index=True)`, logical reference with no cross-module FK), `purpose` (`CharField(max_length=255)`), `check_in_time` (`DateTimeField`), `check_out_time` (`DateTimeField(null=True, blank=True)`), `created_at`, `updated_at`.
  - `Meta.ordering = ['-check_in_time', '-created_at', '-id']` (newest first).
  - `delete()` overridden to raise `PermissionError` (conservative OQ-32 default under BR-05).
- **Module Boundary (`hostel/student_service.py`):**
  - Added `StudentService.student_exists(student_id: str) -> bool` (wrapping `exists(student_id)`) so `operations/visitor_service.py` never queries `Student` directly.
- **Business Logic (`operations/visitor_service.py`):**
  - `create_visitor_entry(visitor_name, student_id, purpose, check_in_time)`: validates non-empty fields and student existence via `StudentService.student_exists()`, rejecting unknown `student_id` (`EC-49` / `AC-18.2`).
  - `record_check_out(entry_id, check_out_time)`: records `check_out_time` on an open entry; rejects attempts to overwrite an existing `check_out_time` or set a time earlier than `check_in_time` (`AC-18.3`).
  - `list_visitor_entries()`: warden-only listing; no delete function and no student-facing read interface exposed (`BR-05`, `BR-13`).
- **Views, URLs & Templates (`operations/views.py`, `operations/urls.py`, `templates/operations/warden_visitor_log.html`, `templates/accounts/warden_dashboard.html`):**
  - `warden_visitor_log`, `warden_visitor_create`, `warden_visitor_checkout` — all guarded with `@warden_required` (`accounts.guards`) under `warden/visitors/`.
  - Enabled the `Visitor Log` link on `templates/accounts/warden_dashboard.html`; no links added to `templates/base.html` or student views (`BR-13`).
