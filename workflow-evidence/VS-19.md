## 1. Scope

Read-only warden pending-items dashboard aggregating four distinct counts strictly through each module's public service interface:
- **Pending Payment Proofs** (`finance.services.count_pending_payments()`)
- **Open Complaints (Submitted / In Progress)** (`operations.complaint_service.ComplaintService.get_pending_count()`)
- **Pending Room Requests** (`hostel.services.get_pending_request_count()`)
- **Pending Leave Requests** (`LeaveService.get_pending_count()` when VS-15 is present; defaults to `0` with `leave_module_available=False` until VS-15 is merged)

Per ADR-005 and `AGENTS.md`, Dashboard owns **no database tables** (`models.py` omitted), never queries another module's tables directly, and **excludes Meal Opt-Outs**.
