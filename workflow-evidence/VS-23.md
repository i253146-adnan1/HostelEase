## Planned Scope
- Presentation-only English (`en`) / Urdu (`ur`) language toggle on the Warden Dashboard.
- Urdu labels for Warden Dashboard cards, headings, and navigation.
- RTL layout support (`dir="rtl"`) when Urdu is active.
- Server-side Django session preference (`ADR-003`) with clean fallback to English (`en`, `dir="ltr"`).
- Zero database schema/model/migration changes, no external translation service, and zero impact on Student views or backend business logic.
